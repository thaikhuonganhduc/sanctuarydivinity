Shipping/Returns

Last updated October 27, 2026

Processing

Every SANCTUARY drop is cut and sewn in limited batches. Orders ship from our studio in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam, and take 1 to 3 business days to process after payment is approved. Orders placed on weekends or holidays start processing the next business day.

Delivery estimates

Estimates start when your order leaves the studio and do not include processing time. They are not guaranteed, and customs can add delays.

  • Vietnam: 2 to 4 business days
  • Asia: 4 to 8 business days
  • Australia and New Zealand: 6 to 12 business days
  • Europe and the United Kingdom: 7 to 14 business days
  • United States and Canada: 7 to 14 business days
  • South America: 10 to 20 business days

Shipping cost

The shipping fee is calculated in your cart from the weight of your pieces and your destination country. Choose your country in Checkout to see the estimate before you pay.

Duties and taxes

Orders shipped outside Vietnam may be charged import duty, VAT or sales tax when they arrive. The cart shows an estimate, but the final amount is set by your local customs and is paid by the recipient. If a parcel is refused or abandoned over unpaid duties, the order is not refunded.

Tracking

You will receive a tracking number by email once your label is created. Tracking can take 24 to 48 hours to update after the carrier first scans your parcel.

Addresses

Please double-check your address before you place the order. We cannot change an address once an order has been processed, and we are not responsible for parcels sent to an incorrect address entered at checkout.

Lost or stolen parcels

Once the carrier marks a parcel as delivered, it is out of our hands. If something looks wrong, contact the carrier first and then write to us with your order number so we can help with the claim.

Returns and exchanges

All sales are final. We do not accept returns or refunds for change of mind, color preference, or fit. Use the Find your size tool on each product page before you buy.

The exception is a piece that arrives damaged, defective, or is not what you ordered. In that case:

  • Email us within 48 hours of delivery at support@sanctuary.example.
  • Include your order number, clear photos of the piece, and a photo of the shipping label and packaging.
  • Claims sent late or without photos cannot be approved.
  • Approved claims are solved with an exchange for the same piece if it is in stock. If it is sold out, we issue store credit.

Cancellations

Orders cannot be cancelled once they are processed. If you made a mistake, write to us as soon as possible and we will try to catch it before the order is packed.

Pre-orders

Sometimes a drop opens for a short time before the pieces are produced. Pre-orders take 3 to 5 weeks to ship, are final sale, and are only replaced if they arrive damaged.

Sold out

Our drops are limited and most pieces never return. Sold out means sold out.

Accessibility

Last updated October 27, 2026

Our commitment

SANCTUARY should be usable by as many people as possible. We aim to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA, and we keep improving the site as we find gaps.

What we do

  • Pages use semantic HTML, with headings, landmarks, and labelled form fields.
  • The site can be used with a keyboard: product cards, size and color buttons, menus, and popups can all be reached and activated, and Esc closes popups.
  • Product images have descriptive text for screen readers.
  • Form errors are written in plain words next to the field.
  • If your device is set to reduce motion, videos stay still and animations are switched off.
  • The layout adapts to phones, tablets, and desktop screens, and text can be zoomed.

Known limits

Our dark design uses light text on a black background, but some small decorative text, such as the red notes on the home page, has lower contrast. Background videos have no sound and carry no information that is not also in the text. We are working on these points.

Need help?

If something on the site is hard to use, or you would like information in another format, email support@sanctuary.example with the page address and a description of the problem. We reply within 2 business days, and we can also take your order by email if the store is not working for you.
